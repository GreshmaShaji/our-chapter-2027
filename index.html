Our Story

✧ ♡ ✧

Who knew a simple “Hi” could turn into a forever? ❤️

Our story began with a little message before he moved to Germany, asking for help with university and settling into a new country. Little did I know that the stranger I was helping would one day become my favourite person.

We finally met at Nuremberg railway station, where I went to pick him up. What started as a simple friendship slowly grew through endless conversations, study sessions, shared dreams, and cheering each other on through life's ups and downs.

Somewhere between the little adventures, struggles, and countless laughs, we found love in the most unexpected way.

I helped him find his way in a new country, and somewhere along the way, we found our home in each other. 🤍

And now, from a simple “Hi” to a lifetime of “I do,” our forever begins!
